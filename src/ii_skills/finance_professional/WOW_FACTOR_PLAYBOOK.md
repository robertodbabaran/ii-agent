## Tier 1: Primary Research (HIGHEST IMPACT)

### 1. Expert Interview Programs
| Year | Scale | Types of Experts |
|------|-------|-----------------|
| 2024 Cargojet | 15 interviews | Consultants, freight association execs, airline leaders, customer CFOs, board directors, law firm partners, economists, ESG consultants |
| 2025 Dom Dev | 30+ interviews | C-suite competitors, World Bank economist, Polish Economic Institute, mortgage consultants, real estate experts, Big 4 professionals, IB professionals, ESG practitioners |

**How to Deploy:**
1. Draft interview guide (10-15 questions) structured around investment thesis pillars
2. Target 5+ interviewees across: industry experts, competitors, customers, consultants
3. Anonymize and directly quote in analysis (weave into every section)
4. Use interviews to validate/challenge key assumptions

**Why It Wows:** Sell-side can't replicate this. Shows genuine intellectual curiosity and hustle.

### 2. Consumer/Customer Surveys
| Year | Scale | Method |
|------|-------|--------|
| 2017 Copa | 385 respondents, 6 regions | Consumer preference survey |
| 2022 MSI | First responders | Body camera preference survey |
| 2024 Cargojet | N=97 | Pre-COVID vs Post-COVID shopping preference |

**How to Deploy:**
Design 10-15 question survey. Distribute via LinkedIn, industry forums, or direct outreach. Minimum N=50 for credibility.

### 3. Physical Site Visits
| Year | What | Insight |
|------|------|---------|
| 2016 Canadian Tire | Store floor plan visits (3 retailers) | Physical competitive analysis |
| 2025 Dom Dev | Construction site + sales office | "30% sold, 4 months in" |

**Why It Wows:** Shows you went beyond the Bloomberg terminal. Creates proprietary data points.

## Tier 2: Quantitative Innovation (HIGH IMPACT)

### 4. Revenue-Weighted WACC Components
- **Risk-free rate** weighted by revenue geography (2021 Vestas): EMEA 1.0%, Americas 2.4%, APAC 3.1% → blended 1.87%
- **Country risk premium** weighted by revenue geography (2017 Copa): 5 countries, each with own CRP
- **Terminal growth** weighted by geographic GDP (2018 Lausanne): Japan, China, USA, EU/CH, Korea

**When to Deploy:** Any company with >30% revenue outside home country.

### 5. Reverse DCF / Market-Implied Analysis
| Year | Insight |
|------|---------|
| 2018 | Market requires 12% FCF growth; we forecast 13% |
| 2020 | Solved for cash rate where ROE = Ke (Effective Lower Bound) |
| 2023 | Market pricing in load factors 2.3-4.6% below minimum historical |
| 2024 | Market pricing 6.0% CAGR; we see 7.5% |
| 2025 | Market pricing 3% CAGR; we see 8% |

**How to Deploy:** Always include. Takes 30 minutes. Explicitly shows degree of mispricing.

### 6. HMSP / Dual-Margin Model (2019 D&L)
Separate higher-margin products from commodity products. Model each margin trajectory independently, then blend by revenue weight.

**When to Deploy:** Companies with mixed product quality (premium + commodity).

### 7. Illiquidity Premium from Academic Literature (2025 Kozminski)
40bps premium calibrated to bid-ask spread using Amihud & Mendelson (1986).

**When to Deploy:** Small/mid-cap companies with low trading volume. Cite the academic paper.

### 8. Black-Scholes for Management Options with Circularity Resolution (2025 Kozminski)
Used treasury stock method to resolve circular reference between option value and share price.

**When to Deploy:** Companies with significant management option programs.

### 9. Brownian Motion Price Path Simulation (2022 MSI)
1,000,000 price paths using geometric Brownian motion from historical returns.
Separate from fundamental Monte Carlo — tests purely statistical price dynamics.

**When to Deploy:** As a cross-check alongside fundamental valuation.

## Tier 3: Analytical Differentiation (MEDIUM IMPACT)

### 10. Proprietary Efficiency Metrics
| Metric | Year | Insight |
|--------|------|---------|
| EBITDA/GW | 2021 Vestas | 120 vs peers 60 (2x more efficient) |
| Price basket index | 2016 Canadian Tire | Price-weighted across 6 retailers |
| Revenue/lb capacity | 2024 Cargojet | Asset productivity metric |
| "Products with DNL grow faster" | 2019 D&L | 11.7% vs 6.7% CAGR |

**How to Deploy:** Create a custom metric that captures the company's competitive advantage in one number.

### 11. Offshore Lifetime Value Analysis (2021 Vestas)
Decompose EBIT contribution over FULL product lifetime (manufacturing 40% + service 60% for offshore). Discount service revenue stream at WACC.

**When to Deploy:** Any company with significant aftermarket/service revenue.

### 12. Replicating Portfolio / Hedging Book Quantification (2020 CBA)
Quantified NIM drag from hedging book roll-off at -3 to -5bp per annum. Most sell-side treats this qualitatively.

**When to Deploy:** Banks and financial institutions with material hedging books.

### 13. Alternative Data Sources
| Data Source | Year | Use |
|-------------|------|-----|
| FlightRadar satellite data | 2024 | Verify LatAm flight activity independently |
| Google Destination Insights | 2023 | Leading indicator of travel demand |
| Web scraping for competitive intel | 2016 | Price comparison data |
| Statistics Canada CANSIM data | 2016 | Subsector category analysis |
| Quarterly bankruptcy tracker | 2025 | Market consolidation thesis |

### 14. Competitive Advantage Flywheel (2021 Vestas)
```
Higher R&D → Superior Technology → Premium Pricing →
Higher Profitability → More R&D Investment → (cycle repeats)
```
Visualize the self-reinforcing cycle. Powerful for companies with technology moats.

### 15. Accretive Acquisition Analysis (2021 Vestas)
Show that acquisition was made at a discount:
```
Company P/E: 59x
Acquisition implied P/E: 31.5x
→ Bought at deep discount, accretive from day 1
```

### 16. Insourcing Break-Even (2024 Cargojet)
Quantify the cost for a customer to replicate the service internally:
```
~25-30% of national domestic volume needed = $413-535M investment
→ Economically impossible for any single customer
→ Moat is permanent
```
