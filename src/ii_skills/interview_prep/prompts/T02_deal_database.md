## Instructions

Build a **2,000-3,000 word** database of {{COMPANY_NAME}}'s deals and transactions. Use provided URLs, press releases, and web searches to identify recent activity.

### Required Sections

#### 1. Recent Closed Transactions (Last 2-3 Years)
Create a table with columns:
| Date | Target/Asset | Transaction Type | Size/EV | Sector | Key Details |

Include:
- Acquisitions / investments made
- Exits / dispositions
- Fund closings
- Notable financing transactions

#### 2. Active / Announced Deals
- Deals currently in market or recently announced
- Mandates or advisory roles
- Pipeline signals from press coverage

#### 3. Notable Exits & Returns
- Successful exits with known multiples or IRR
- Timeline from investment to exit
- Value creation narrative

#### 4. Deal Themes & Patterns
- Preferred deal size range
- Sector focus areas
- Geographic preferences
- Deal sourcing patterns (proprietary vs. auction)
- Typical hold period
- Value creation playbook themes

#### 5. Interview-Ready Deal Discussion Points
- 2-3 deals you could discuss in detail
- For each: what was interesting, what drove returns, what risks existed
- How these connect to the {{ROLE_TITLE}} role

---

## Output Format
Use tables for deal data. Write narrative analysis for themes and discussion points. Include specific dollar amounts, dates, and names wherever available.
