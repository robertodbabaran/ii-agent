## Instructions

Research {{COMPANY_NAME}} comprehensively and produce a **3,000-5,000 word** deep dive covering the sections below. Use the provided URLs as primary sources, supplemented by web searches for recent news and filings.

### Required Sections

#### 1. Firm Overview (500-800 words)
- What does the firm do? Core business lines, AUM/revenue, founding date, HQ
- Ownership structure (public/private, parent company, fund structure)
- Key financial metrics (AUM, fund sizes, deal count, revenue if public)

#### 2. Leadership & Key People (400-600 words)
- CEO/Managing Partners and their backgrounds
- Head of {{TARGET_FUNCTION}} team
- Any interviewers: {{INTERVIEWER_NAMES}}
- Recent leadership changes or notable hires

#### 3. Corporate Structure & Strategy (400-600 words)
- Business segments/divisions
- Geographic footprint
- Growth strategy (organic, acquisitions, new fund launches)
- Recent strategic shifts

#### 4. Culture & Values (300-500 words)
- Stated values and mission
- Glassdoor/reputation signals
- Employee reviews themes
- Diversity & inclusion initiatives

#### 5. Competitive Positioning (400-600 words)
- Key competitors and how {{COMPANY_NAME}} differentiates
- Market position within its segment
- Competitive advantages (brand, relationships, expertise)
- Vulnerabilities or challenges

#### 6. Recent News & Developments (400-600 words)
- Last 6-12 months of significant news
- New fund launches, major deals, leadership changes
- Media coverage themes
- Regulatory or legal developments

---

## Output Format
Write in clear, professional prose with bold headers. Include specific data points (dollar amounts, dates, names) wherever possible. Flag any information that could not be verified.
