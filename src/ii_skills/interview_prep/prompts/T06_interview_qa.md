## Instructions

Produce a **2,000-3,000 word** interview Q&A preparation framework. Each question should include a structured answer framework, not a scripted answer.

### Required Sections

#### 1. Top 10 Most Likely Questions (Ranked by Probability)

For each question provide:
- **The Question** (exact wording an interviewer would use)
- **Why They Ask This** (what they're really testing)
- **Answer Framework** (3-4 bullet structure to hit)
- **Key Data Points to Include** (specific facts about {{COMPANY_NAME}})
- **Pitfalls to Avoid**

Categories to cover:
- "Walk me through your resume" / background
- "Why {{COMPANY_NAME}}?" / "Why this role?"
- Technical/domain expertise
- Situational judgment
- Culture fit

#### 2. Technical Questions (5 Questions)

For each:
- **Question** with expected difficulty level
- **Model Answer Structure** with key concepts to reference
- **Common Mistakes** candidates make

Tailor to {{TARGET_FUNCTION}} — could include:
- Financial modeling, valuation, market analysis
- Industry-specific technical knowledge
- Process/workflow questions

#### 3. Behavioral Questions — STAR Format (5 Questions)

For each:
- **Question** (e.g., "Tell me about a time you...")
- **STAR Framework:**
  - **Situation:** What context to set
  - **Task:** What was your specific responsibility
  - **Action:** What you did (be specific)
  - **Result:** Quantified outcome
- **Which Background Point to Use** from your experience

#### 4. Questions to Ask the Interviewer (10 Questions)

Organized by category:
- **Role & Team** (3 questions)
- **Strategy & Growth** (3 questions)
- **Culture & Development** (2 questions)
- **Impressive / Insightful** (2 questions that show deep research)

For each: explain *why* it's a strong question and what signal it sends.

---

## Output Format
Use headers and bullet points for easy scanning during prep. Bold key phrases and data points. This should be a working document the candidate reviews the night before.
