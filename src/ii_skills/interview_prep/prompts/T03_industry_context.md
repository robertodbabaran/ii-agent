## Instructions

Produce a **1,500-2,500 word** industry analysis for the market in which {{COMPANY_NAME}} operates. This should demonstrate deep understanding of the competitive landscape and key dynamics during an interview.

### Required Sections

#### 1. Market Overview (300-500 words)
- Industry definition and scope
- Total addressable market (TAM) with size and growth rate
- Key market segments
- Where {{COMPANY_NAME}} fits within this market

#### 2. Key Trends (3-5 Trends, 200-300 words each)
For each trend:
- What is happening and why
- Quantified impact (dollar values, growth rates, adoption metrics)
- How it affects {{COMPANY_NAME}} specifically
- Timeline and maturity of the trend

#### 3. Competitive Landscape (300-500 words)
- Major players and their positioning
- Market share or ranking data
- {{COMPANY_NAME}}'s relative position
- Barriers to entry and switching costs

#### 4. Regulatory Environment (200-300 words)
- Key regulations affecting the industry
- Recent or upcoming regulatory changes
- Compliance considerations for {{TARGET_FUNCTION}}

#### 5. Forward Outlook (200-400 words)
- 12-24 month industry outlook
- Bull and bear scenarios
- What a thoughtful candidate should watch for
- Implications for the {{ROLE_TITLE}} role

---

## Output Format
Write in analytical prose suitable for someone preparing for a senior interview. Include specific data points, cite recent reports or articles where possible, and distinguish between established facts and forward-looking views.
