## Instructions

Produce a **1,000-1,500 word** stakeholder analysis for {{COMPANY_NAME}}. Focus on the audiences and relationships most relevant to the {{TARGET_FUNCTION}} function.

### Required Sections

#### 1. Investor Base / Client Profile
- Who are the key investors, LPs, or clients?
- Institutional vs. retail breakdown
- Notable anchor investors or strategic partners
- AUM concentration or client diversification

#### 2. Key Relationships
- Board of directors (names, affiliations, relevance)
- Banking relationships (lenders, advisors, prime brokers)
- Key service providers (legal, accounting, consulting)
- Industry associations and affiliations
- Government or regulatory relationships

#### 3. Geographic Distribution
- Where are the investors/clients based?
- Regional focus areas
- Cross-border considerations
- Expansion targets

#### 4. What Stakeholders Care About
- Top priorities for each stakeholder group
- Common questions or concerns
- Reporting cadence and expectations
- Hot-button issues or sensitivities
- How the {{ROLE_TITLE}} interacts with these stakeholders

---

## Output Format
Write in analytical prose with bullet points for specific names and data. Focus on information that would help someone in the {{ROLE_TITLE}} role understand who they serve and what matters to those people.
