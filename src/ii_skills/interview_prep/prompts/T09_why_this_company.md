## Instructions

Craft a **"Why {{COMPANY_NAME}}?"** response that is under **400 words** and can be delivered naturally in **60-90 seconds**. This is the single most important answer to nail — it must feel genuine, specific, and data-backed.

### Three-Part Structure

#### Part 1: Intellectual Rationale (~120 words)
Why does {{COMPANY_NAME}}'s work interest you intellectually?
- Reference a specific deal, strategy, or market position
- Show you understand what makes the firm distinctive
- Include at least one quantified data point
- Avoid generic praise ("great firm", "impressive track record")

#### Part 2: Value Fit Rationale (~120 words)
Why is there a cultural or philosophical alignment?
- Connect a specific firm value or approach to your own experience
- Reference something from the website, a talk, an article, or an employee's background
- Be authentic — pick something that genuinely resonates
- Tie to your background: {{BACKGROUND_POINTS}}

#### Part 3: Timing Rationale (~120 words)
Why now? Why is this the right moment for both sides?
- Reference a current initiative, fund launch, or growth phase
- Explain what you bring that's particularly valuable right now
- Show awareness of where the firm is headed
- End with forward-looking energy, not backward-looking gratitude

### Quality Checks
- [ ] Contains at least 2 specific data points about {{COMPANY_NAME}}
- [ ] References something not on the careers page (shows deeper research)
- [ ] Connects your background to their needs
- [ ] Avoids cliches and generic language
- [ ] Can be spoken naturally in under 90 seconds
- [ ] Ends on a forward-looking note

---

## Output Format

Write the response in natural spoken language (not bullets). Then add a **"Key data points referenced"** section listing the specific facts used, with sources. Finally, add **delivery notes** (where to pause, what to emphasize).
