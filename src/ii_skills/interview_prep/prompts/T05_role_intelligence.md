## Instructions

Produce a **1,000-1,500 word** analysis of the {{ROLE_TITLE}} role at {{COMPANY_NAME}}, synthesizing the job description with market intelligence to understand what the hiring team really wants.

### Required Sections

#### 1. Team Structure & Reporting
- Where does this role sit in the org chart?
- Who does the role report to?
- How large is the {{TARGET_FUNCTION}} team?
- Cross-functional partnerships (which teams does this role work with?)

#### 2. Day-to-Day Responsibilities
- Core deliverables and workflows
- How time is typically allocated (% breakdown)
- Tools, platforms, and systems likely used
- Travel expectations

#### 3. Success Metrics
**First 90 Days:**
- Quick wins the hiring manager likely expects
- Onboarding milestones
- Relationship-building priorities

**First Year:**
- Key performance indicators
- Projects or initiatives to own
- Measurable impact expected

#### 4. Growth Trajectory
- Typical career progression from this role
- Timeline for advancement
- Skills to develop for next level
- Examples of people who have held similar roles

#### 5. Compensation Context
- Market range for this role level (if publicly available)
- Typical bonus structure and carry considerations
- Benefits or perks notable for the firm

#### 6. Fit Analysis
Based on the background points provided, identify:
- Strongest alignment points between your experience and the role
- Potential gaps to address proactively
- Unique value-add you bring vs. other candidates

---

## Output Format
Write in clear, actionable prose. Be specific about what the role entails and how to position yourself. Where information is speculative (not from the JD), label it as an informed inference.
