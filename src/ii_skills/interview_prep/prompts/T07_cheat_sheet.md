## Instructions

Produce a **compact, scannable cheat sheet** (~1,000 words) designed to be reviewed in the 30 minutes before the interview. Use bullet points exclusively — no paragraphs.

### Required Sections

#### Key Statistics
- AUM / Revenue / Market Cap
- Number of employees / team size
- Fund sizes (current and prior vintages)
- Number of portfolio companies / assets
- Geographic footprint (offices, markets)
- Year founded, ownership structure

#### Important Names & Titles
- CEO / Managing Partners
- Head of {{TARGET_FUNCTION}}
- Your interviewers: {{INTERVIEWER_NAMES}}
- Recent notable hires or departures
- Board members (if relevant)

#### Recent Deal Data Points
- 3-5 most recent/notable transactions
- For each: target, size, date, one-line significance
- Current pipeline or announced deals

#### Firm-Specific Terminology
- Internal jargon or brand names for processes
- Fund names and vintage years
- Proprietary frameworks or methodologies
- Acronyms used in job description or website

#### Dates & Timelines
- Upcoming catalysts (fund closings, earnings, events)
- Recent milestones (last 6 months)
- Industry events or conferences
- Regulatory deadlines

#### Your Talking Points
Based on: {{BACKGROUND_POINTS}}
- 3 strongest alignment points with this role
- 2 stories ready to tell (with quantified outcomes)
- 1 "why now" personal motivation statement

## Output Format
Pure bullet points. Use **bold** for data points and names. Group tightly — this is a reference card, not a narrative.
